Gaming Quiz Extension Configuration

About the Extension

The Gaming Quiz extension allows streamers to engage with their viewers through interactive gaming quizzes.

Viewers can test their gaming knowledge with questions about different games, characters, and gaming history.

How to Use

No configuration is needed. The extension works out of the box.

Viewers will need to enter their own RapidAPI key to use the extension.

They can get a free key at: https://rapidapi.com/devsdocs/api/game-quiz
